Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password, along with the expense records you create — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate Spendly: authenticating your account, storing your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your financial data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We retain your data for as long as your account remains active.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. Any third-party services we rely on for core functionality (such as hosting or email delivery) are bound to handle your data securely.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through your account settings or support channel.
